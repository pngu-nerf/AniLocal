# Code of Conduct

AniLocal follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and constructive; harassment, personal attacks and
discriminatory language are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions
or anywhere else this project is worked on. Maintainers may edit, remove or
decline contributions and comments that do not meet this standard, and may
block repeat offenders.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/pngu-nerf/AniLocal/security/advisories/new)
(the same private channel `SECURITY.md` uses — it reaches only the
maintainer). Reports are read and answered; the reporter's privacy is kept.

The full text of the Covenant, including the enforcement ladder, is at the
link above and applies here.
